Project Overview

Deepslice is a pizza delivery mobile application designed to provide users with a seamless ordering experience. The app allows customers to browse menu items, customize pizzas, place orders, and track deliveries in real time.

The project focused on improving usability, reducing friction during ordering, and creating a visually engaging experience that encourages repeat purchases.

The Problem

Ordering food should be quick and effortless, but many food delivery apps create unnecessary friction through complex navigation, overwhelming menus, and lengthy checkout processes.

  • • Complex Navigation: Users struggled to quickly find their preferred items among large menus and categories.
  • • Lengthy Checkout Process: Multiple steps during checkout increased drop-offs and delayed order completion.
  • • Poor Order Visibility: Customers wanted clearer updates about order preparation and delivery status.

The Objective

The primary objective was to create a pizza ordering experience that feels simple, intuitive, and enjoyable.
